About Dhara

Wellness, without the rush

Dhara opened in Koramangala with one idea at its centre: that a good therapy session shouldn't feel like an appointment squeezed into your day. It should feel like the one part of the day nobody's rushing you through.

Our therapists are trained across Ayurvedic and international technique, and every room is kept quiet, private, and unhurried — from the moment you're welcomed in to the moment you step back out onto the street.
